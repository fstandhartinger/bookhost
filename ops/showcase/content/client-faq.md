# Client FAQ — sample answers

This fictional FAQ shows how Northwind Studio keeps approved answers close to its project work.

## What happens after the kickoff?

We confirm the goals, audience, decision makers, and first review date in the project brief. The project coordinator sends a written recap and flags anything that still needs approval.

## How should we send feedback?

Use the agreed review document and group comments by page or section. One person on the client team should collect conflicting feedback before the next review.

## Can the timeline change?

Yes. A change to scope, assets, or approval timing can affect delivery. We record the change, its owner, and the revised checkpoint before promising a new date.

## What is included in handoff?

The fictional sample package includes approved design files, a short usage guide, and a list of open follow-ups. Hosting, paid media, and ongoing maintenance are separate unless the brief says otherwise.

## Who can approve a change?

The named project decision maker confirms client-facing scope. Northwind Studio records that approval on the project page so the team can refer back to it.
