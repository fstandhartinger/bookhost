# Onboarding notes for the Lantern desk

These notes describe the onboarding arrangement for the fictional Lantern desk at the fictional company Mossquay. They were written on 2026-08-12 as a handover between the coordinator and the colleague covering the welcome shift. They are working notes, not a promise that access will be ready when someone arrives. The coordinator should explain that distinction before handing over the welcome folder.

## Before arrival

The coordinator creates a welcome ticket 5 working days before the start date. The ticket contains the preferred display name, the team, and the requested equipment. Personal documents must not be attached to this ticket. The equipment cupboard currently contains 7 keyboards and 4 headsets. Those numbers describe the last stock check and do not guarantee availability for the next arrival. A missing item should stay visible in the ticket until the coordinator has confirmed a replacement.

Access to the shared workspace needs approval from the team lead. The coordinator may request access but cannot approve their own request. The identity check happens separately, and its result should be recorded as complete or pending without copying identity documents into the workspace. There is no confirmed deadline for that check in these notes.

## Welcome shift

The welcome session starts at 09:30 and lasts 45 minutes. The colleague explains how to find the handbook, where to ask operational questions, and how to report equipment problems. The new starter then opens the handbook using their own account. A successful demonstration on somebody else's account does not count as access verification.

## Follow-up

On day 3, the coordinator asks whether the starter can find the rota and submit a practice ticket. The practice ticket must be marked as training so that it does not enter the customer queue. Outstanding permissions remain assigned to the team lead for review; the notes do not name a backup approver.

The team still needs to decide who covers the welcome shift during leave, whether remote starters receive a separate equipment checklist, and where completed welcome tickets should be archived. None of those questions was settled in the handover. The reviewer should confirm them before turning these notes into the permanent onboarding page.
