# Northwind Studio handbook

Northwind Studio is a fictional eight-person brand and web design studio used for BookHost product demonstrations. It helps independent food and hospitality businesses make their websites and customer materials easier to use.

## How we work

- Keep project decisions in the client project book so the whole team can find them.
- Write down the decision, the owner, and the next check-in when a meeting changes scope.
- Ask before promising a date or a deliverable that is not in the approved brief.
- Share review links with the smallest group that needs them.
- Use fictional examples in training and public demonstrations. Never copy client records into this workspace.

## Studio roles

- **Mira Chen, Studio Lead:** project direction, scope, and client approvals.
- **Sam Patel, Design Lead:** visual systems, design reviews, and handoff quality.
- **Avery Brooks, Project Coordinator:** timelines, meeting notes, and open questions.
- **Jordan Kim, Research Lead:** interview plans and synthesis.

All names, clients, dates, and work in this handbook are fictional.

## Where information belongs

- Team Handbook: studio agreements and onboarding.
- Client Projects: approved briefs, review notes, and delivery checklists.
- Operations: release, access, and continuity procedures.

## Before sharing a page

Confirm its audience, remove personal details, check that links point to the intended project, and ask the page owner to review changes that affect a client commitment.
