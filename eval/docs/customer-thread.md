# Customer enquiry thread: Fernwharf workspace

This is a fictional email thread between the Fernwharf cooperative and the support desk at Alderquill. The messages concern a possible workspace migration. The customer has not placed an order. The thread is kept as reference material for preparing a response, and the eventual knowledge page should clearly distinguish customer requests from confirmed product behaviour.

## Customer message, 2026-08-21

We currently maintain 18 handbook pages and would like to bring them into a shared workspace. There are 9 colleagues who may need access, although we have not decided who should be able to publish. Can you confirm whether our existing page tables will survive the migration? Some tables contain responsibility assignments, so a list of disconnected cell values would not be useful to us.

We would also like to know whether a reviewer can compare an uploaded document with the suggested page before approving it. Our internal process requires that comparison. We are asking about a possible trial rather than requesting an immediate migration. Please do not interpret this message as approval to transfer our existing files.

## Support reply, 2026-08-22

Thank you for explaining the review requirement. A human approval step is part of the proposed workflow. We still need to inspect a representative sample before confirming how your tables will be handled. Please send a fictional or redacted example through the agreed intake channel. Do not send account passwords or confidential customer records in a reply to this thread.

The support desk has not confirmed a migration date, price, service availability target, or contractual data location. Those topics need a separate response after the sample review. The approval workflow statement should not be expanded into claims about features that have not been discussed.

## Customer follow-up, 2026-08-24

We will prepare a redacted example. Our preferred migration window begins on 2026-09-07, but that is a preference, not an agreed appointment. We also need to identify the person who can approve the trial internally. Please keep the enquiry open while we arrange that approval.
