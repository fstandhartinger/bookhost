# Bracken pilot meeting

The fictional Bracken team met on 2026-08-18 to review a small internal document pilot. The discussion covered the pilot boundary, the review queue, and the evidence needed before expansion. These notes combine the facilitator's record with written corrections from the participants. They are intended for the internal handbook and do not describe a public launch commitment.

## Pilot boundary

The team reviewed 24 sample documents from the training collection. Of these, 6 contained incomplete ownership information. The sample was deliberately selected to expose gaps and cannot be used to estimate error rates across the whole organisation. No customer documents were included. Participants agreed that the next round should remain inside the training collection until the ownership questions are resolved.

## Decisions

Decision: keep the pilot limited to the support handbook. Decision: require a human reviewer before any generated page is published. Decision: retain the original source beside the proposed page so that reviewers can compare wording and figures. These decisions apply immediately to the pilot; they do not establish a company-wide retention policy.

The proposed expansion to the engineering handbook was postponed. A participant suggested a target in September, but the meeting did not approve a date. The proposal to publish drafts automatically was rejected because the review queue still contained ambiguous source material. A rejected proposal must not appear as an approved action in the resulting handbook page.

## Actions

The facilitator will collect the missing ownership information by 2026-08-25. The support lead will review the wording of the approval checklist before the next pilot session. No deadline was assigned to that wording review. The operations group will test whether the source attachment remains accessible after publication and report the result in the pilot ticket.

## Open questions

The meeting did not assign a permanent owner for the review queue. It also left the location of the pilot archive undecided. Participants requested clarification about how to record disagreements between reviewers without overwriting the earlier comments. No budget, supplier contract, or external audit was approved. The reviewer of this page should distinguish the agreed pilot rules from the postponed expansion, check the action deadline against the ticket, and confirm which questions still need an owner.
